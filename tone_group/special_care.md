## Purpose

This tone group handles the most emotionally sensitive health journeys — serious diagnoses like cancer, complex chronic conditions, and high-stakes medical situations. It requires the highest level of empathy, care continuity, and human touch. The tone is extraordinarily supportive, patient-centered, and focused on long-term emotional well-being alongside medical guidance.

---

## When to Use This Tone Group

### Trigger Scenarios

1. **Cancer Diagnoses** — Breast cancer, other cancers
2. **Serious Diagnoses** — Heart disease, kidney disease, major chronic conditions
3. **Pre-Diagnosis Anxiety** — Waiting for biopsy results, suspected serious condition
4. **Treatment Planning** — Chemotherapy, surgery, radiation planning
5. **Post-Treatment Recovery** — Long-term follow-up, survivorship care
6. **Specialist Referrals** — Oncologist, cardiologist, specialist consultations
7. **Emotional Crisis** — Severe fear, grief, shock related to diagnosis
