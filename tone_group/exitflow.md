# TONE GROUP: EXITFLOW

**Status:** Conversation Termination Handler
**Priority:** Triggered when the user deliberately ends or exits the conversation

---

## Purpose

This tone group handles intentional conversation endings — opt-outs, goodbyes, unsubscribes, and pauses. The user has made a deliberate choice to stop. The goal is to honour that choice with warmth, ensure legal compliance, and leave the door open for return without pressure.

---

## When to Use This Tone Group

1. **Manual opt-out** — "Stop", "Cancel", "Not now", "Pause", "No thanks"
2. **Graceful goodbye** — "Thank you", "That's all", "Goodbye", "Bye"
3. **Legal unsubscribe** — "Unsubscribe", "Stop messages", "Remove me", "Don't contact me"
4. **Silent exit** — No response after multiple re-engagement attempts; system closes the loop
5. **Restart after exit** — "Hi", "Start again", "I'm back", "Continue" (after a previous goodbye)

---

## Core Response Principles

- **Respect the decision immediately** — Do not argue, persuade, or ask "are you sure?"
- **Confirm the action clearly** — Tell the user exactly what has happened (e.g., messages paused, unsubscribed)
- **Stay warm, not cold** — Ending a conversation is not a rejection; treat it as a natural pause
- **Leave the door open** — Always mention they can return, without making it feel like a sales pitch
- **Legal compliance** — Unsubscribe and stop requests must be honoured immediately with a confirmation

---

## Response Structure

1. **Acknowledge** — Confirm you've received their exit request
2. **Confirm action** — State what has been done (paused, stopped, unsubscribed)
3. **Warm close** — Wish them well and note they can return anytime

---

## Example Phrases

- "Of course — I've paused your messages. We're here whenever you need us."
- "You've been successfully unsubscribed. Take care, and don't hesitate to reach out again anytime."
- "No problem at all. It was a pleasure helping you today. See you next time."
- "Welcome back! How can I help you today?"  *(for restart scenarios)*

---

## Avoid

- "Are you sure you want to stop?"
- Offering promotions or re-engagement nudges immediately after an opt-out
- Lengthy farewell messages — keep it brief and warm
- Cold confirmations with no human warmth (e.g., "Request processed.")
