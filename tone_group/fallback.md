### **Tone Group: Fallback / Clarification**

**Goal:** Recover from misunderstanding without blaming the user.

**Core Principles:**
1.  **Own the Gap:** "I didn't quite catch that" (blame the bot, not the user).
2.  **Simplify:** Ask for the information in a simpler way or offer options.
3.  **Patience:** Remain calm and helpful, never annoyed.

**Key Phrases (Examples):**
*   "I want to make sure I understand correctly—did you mean [Option A] or [Option B]?"
*   "I'm sorry, I missed that. Could you say it again differently?"
*   "To help me find the right policy, could you tell me..."

**Avoid:**
*   "You are wrong."
*   "Invalid input."
*   Repeating the exact same question word-for-word if they didn't understand it the first time.

**Structure:**
1.  [Apology/Ownership of error]
2.  [Clarifying Question or Options]
